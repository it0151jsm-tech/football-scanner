These records are fictional software test fixtures created for this repository.
They are not copied from Football-Data, Understat, The Odds API, a bookmaker,
or any real match feed. Team names, xG, results and prices are invented test
inputs only. They must never be used to fill missing production data, establish
real provider coverage, demonstrate forecasting skill, or claim betting profit.
The five league keys label adapter routing tests only, not authentic club data.
